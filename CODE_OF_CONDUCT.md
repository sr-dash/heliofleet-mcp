# Code of conduct

heliofleet-mcp adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part, in issues, discussions, pull requests or anywhere else the project meets,
is expected to follow it and to help keep the project welcoming and useful to the heliophysics
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the field, to open source or to the
  language of the discussion.
- Giving and receiving constructive criticism about the work, not the person.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Crediting the work, data and ideas of others.
- Respecting that people contribute in their own time and in different ways.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity, background, career stage or
  institution.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the GitHub repository and its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and any other channel where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please write to the maintainer, Soumyaranjan
Dash, at <sdash@nso.edu>. Reports are read in confidence, and the privacy of the person reporting is
respected. The maintainer will follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), according to the impact of the behaviour.
