# Citing

If heliofleet-mcp helps your research, please cite it, together with the data it reads.

!!! quote "heliofleet-mcp"

    Dash, S. (2026). *heliofleet-mcp: spacecraft geometry and solar magnetic connectivity from
    HelioFleet and HelioField data* [Computer software]. Zenodo.
    <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24409982>

That DOI always resolves to the latest release; each release also has its own DOI on the
[Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24409982). GitHub's **Cite this repository** button
gives APA and BibTeX from
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/sr-dash/heliofleet-mcp/blob/main/CITATION.cff).

## Data sources

- **Positions and geometry:** Dash, S. (2026). *HelioFleet: an interactive map of heliophysics
  spacecraft positions* [Computer software]. Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23328187>.
  Ephemerides: JPL Horizons.
- **Coronal field and connectivity:** HelioField, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23487231>, and the
  magnetogram (NSO/GONG or SDO/HMI).
